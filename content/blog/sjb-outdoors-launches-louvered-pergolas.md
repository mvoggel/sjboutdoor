We're excited to share that SJB Outdoors is now designing and installing
**louvered pergolas** across Florida. It's the product our customers have been
asking about most, and after a year of installs in Naples and Fort Myers we're
ready to make it a core part of what we do.

## What a louvered pergola actually is

Think of it as a roof you control. The louvers — the slats overhead — rotate
from fully open to fully closed:

- **Open** for full sun and airflow.
- **Angled** to cut glare while keeping the breeze.
- **Closed** to shed a sudden Florida downpour and keep the space dry.

Most of our systems are motorized and tie into a rain sensor, so the roof closes
itself when the weather turns. Add integrated lighting and side screens and a
patio becomes a true second living room you can use year-round.

## Why we added it

For years we've focused on shades, shutters, and awnings — products that solve
one problem really well. A louvered pergola does something different: it changes
how much of the year you can actually *use* your outdoor space. In our climate,
that's the difference between a patio that sits empty all summer and one that's
the best room in the house.

> The first question almost everyone asks is "does it really keep the rain out?"
> Closed, yes — the louvers overlap and channel water to a hidden gutter in the
> frame.
